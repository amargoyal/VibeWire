INSTALL VIBEWIRE

1. Drag VibeWire onto Applications in this window.
2. Eject this disk image.
3. Open VibeWire from Applications. Look for its icon in the menu bar.
4. Follow Setup guide to allow Screen Recording and Accessibility, then pair
   your phone. Your computer shares its screen; your phone is the viewer.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

This release is not notarized by Apple. macOS may block the first launch.
If you trust this download, use System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Open Anyway after attempting to open the app. Do not disable Gatekeeper.
More information: https://support.apple.com/102445

Download updates and report problems:
https://github.com/amargoyal/VibeWire
