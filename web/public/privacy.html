Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026

VibeWire lets you see and control your own computer from a phone or a browser. This policy explains what the optional VibeWire account keeps about you, and what it never keeps.

What stays on your devices

The picture of your screen, your pointer and keyboard input, your clipboard, and Claude conversations travel directly between your computer and the device you paired. They are never sent to a VibeWire account or stored by us.

What the account stores

  • Your email address and display name.
  • The computers you paired with: their name, platform and address.
  • The browsers you signed in from.
  • Your keyboard bar and a few client preferences.

Private keys, pairing codes, screen images and transcripts are never stored in the account.

Signing in with Google

If you continue with Google, Google tells us your email address and, when you allow it, your name and profile picture. We use them only to identify your account. We do not receive your password, contacts or any other data from your Google account.

How we use it

Account data is used only to sign you in and to bring your computers and settings to a new browser. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to track you. VibeWire has no ads and no analytics.

Where it is kept

Accounts are hosted by Supabase, which stores the data and sends sign-in emails on our behalf. Every table is protected so that an account can read and change only its own rows.

Deleting your data

You can use VibeWire without an account. To delete an account and everything it stores, open an issue at github.com/amargoyal/VibeWire/issues asking for deletion, and it will be removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
