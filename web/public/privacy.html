Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026

VibeWire lets you see and control your own computer from a phone or a browser. This policy explains what the optional VibeWire account keeps about you, and what it never keeps.

What stays on your devices

The picture of your screen, your pointer and keyboard input, your clipboard, and Claude conversations travel directly between your computer and the device you paired. They are never sent to a VibeWire account or stored by us.
